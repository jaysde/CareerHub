# CareerHub

**Your AI mentor for lifelong learning.**

CareerHub is an AI-powered learning and mentorship platform designed to help people explore subjects, build meaningful skills, and move from curiosity toward real-world goals—all through a more focused learning journey.

## Our Vision

Learning often means jumping between courses, videos, books, and websites without a clear plan. CareerHub aims to bring structure and guidance to that experience through a personalized AI mentor that helps learners decide what to learn, discover useful resources, practice concepts, and keep progressing at their own pace.

CareerHub is designed to **complement educators, not replace them**.

## What CareerHub Aims to Offer

- **Personalized learning paths** — plans shaped around each learner’s goals, current level, and pace.
- **Curated learning resources** — discover books, video lectures, and other useful materials.
- **Notes and learning support** — organize key concepts and make complex topics easier to revisit.
- **Interactive practice** — assignments, quizzes, and exercises to reinforce understanding.
- **AI mentorship** — ask questions, explore ideas, and get guidance whenever it is needed.
- **Career-focused growth** — connect learning with practical skills and real-world projects.

## The Learning Journey

1. **Explore** a subject, interest, or goal.
2. **Learn** with a structured plan and relevant resources.
3. **Practice** through exercises, assignments, and quizzes.
4. **Grow** by applying knowledge and building practical skills.

## Our Philosophy

We believe quality learning should be more accessible, personalized, and connected to the goals people care about. An AI mentor can offer guidance between classes, help learners practice independently, and make it easier to keep moving forward—while teachers and educators remain an important part of the learning journey.

## The Road Ahead

CareerHub’s long-term vision is to support learners throughout their journey, from discovering a new subject to developing skills they can apply in the real world. The experience is intended to grow over time, with a web platform as the starting point and mobile experiences as a future possibility.
